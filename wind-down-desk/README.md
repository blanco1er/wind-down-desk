# Wind-Down Desk

A seated, hands-first evening ritual for Meta Quest 3 / 3S, in mixed reality.
Say what is on your mind; an agent turns each worry into a stone on your real
table. Sort the stones by hand into **Tomorrow**, **Later** or **Let go**.
Let go dissolves a stone into light and adds a star to your own night sky.
The agent proposes a gentle plan for tomorrow, which you keep with a touch,
and it comes back in the morning.

**v6: three rituals on the same tray.** The lamp proposes one from the time,
tonight and what it hears ("You sound tense. Shall we breathe first?"), you
decide by voice or on a glass menu by the lamp: **Unload** (above),
**Breathe** (the lamp becomes a breathing moon, 4 s in / 6 s out) and
**Release** (one heavy thing becomes a dark engraved stone that you let sink
into still black water).

Built with the [Immersive Web SDK](https://iwsdk.dev) (IWSDK 1.0.1, MIT).

## Run

Node `>=20.19 <21 || >=22.12 <23 || >=24` (type stripping is used by the
server and the tests, so Node 22.12+ or 24 is recommended).

```sh
npm install
npm run server       # agent + voice server on 127.0.0.1:8787 (optional)
npm run dev          # managed browser + IWER emulator, https://localhost:8081/
npm run check        # typecheck + 67 tests + production build
```

Without the server the app runs the same agent on the device (simulated,
deterministic), so a static hosting of `dist/` still works end to end.

### Providers (environment only, no secrets in files)

| Variable | Effect |
|---|---|
| `DEEPSEEK_API_KEY` (`DEEPSEEK_MODEL`, `DEEPSEEK_BASE_URL`) | DeepSeek chat model for extraction and planning |
| `LLM_BASE_URL` + `LLM_API_KEY` (`LLM_MODEL`) | any OpenAI-compatible endpoint |
| `STT_BASE_URL` + `STT_API_KEY` (`STT_MODEL`, `STT_LANGUAGE`) | OpenAI-compatible `/audio/transcriptions` |
| `WDD_FORCE_SIMULATED=1` | force the simulated agent and speech-to-text |

With nothing set, both are simulated and no paid call is made. Nebius hosts
are refused by policy. Every LLM answer is validated and clamped; on timeout or
junk the agent falls back to the on-device one.

## The ritual

1. **Arrive** (about 2 s): the mat is laid on the closest real table within
   seated reach (WebXR plane detection, labels from the Quest scene model).
   No table: a floating desk at seated height, and the caption says so.
2. **Brain dump**: hands-free microphone (energy VAD, one clip per sentence,
   server transcription). Each worry drops onto the desk as a stone with a
   short title. No microphone: touching the little lamp (the voice orb) loads an example evening.
3. **Sort by hand**: stones are `OneHandGrabbable` with hand pinch; their grab
   volume is inflated by 2 cm (no rendering cost); a held stone lights the
   bowl it would land in (the agent's suggested bowl breathes faintly); release snaps within 13 cm and up to 24 cm above the
   desk; misses glide home.
4. **Voice for every gesture**: "dentist, tomorrow", "let go of that comment",
   "this one, later" (head gaze), "undo", "that's it", "keep it",
   "make it lighter", "stop listening", "good night", "sort them for me".
5. **Let go**: a short hold, an ember wakes in the bowl, the stone warms, lifts
   and burns away from the rim into rising warm dust; a new star appears;
   spatial sound from the bowl. Recurring worries are noticed.
6. **Tomorrow**: the agent writes up to three tiny first steps with times. A
   touch on "Keep this plan" (or "keep it") saves it; nothing is saved without it.
7. **Night and morning**: streak, stars, and the kept plan returns between
   05:00 and 12:00. "Later" items come back after a week.
8. **Places** (visionOS-style environments): your room by default (passthrough);
   "Places" next to the lamp, or "take me to the lake", "go to the garden",
   "show me the stars", "back to my room". A 1.6 s crossfade; your real table
   stays visible ("full immersion" to be fully there). Panoramas are loaded only
   when chosen (KTX2 / Basis ETC1S, 6144 × 2048 equal-area, 1.4–1.7 MB each,
   6 MiB of GPU memory once transcoded, released when you leave).
   **Provenance:** the three panoramas are **AI-generated** images made for this
   project by Astra (the studio's image agent, an OpenAI image model run through
   Codex) from our written briefs (`../concept-lieux/`), then upscaled ×4
   locally with Real-ESRGAN (BSD-3-Clause), seam and zenith corrected by
   `tools/make-places.py`. No photo of a real place. (v4 used CC0 Poly Haven HDRIs,
   kept in `archive/environments-v4/`.)
   **v8:** the Places menu is a 3 × 2 grid (your room + five places; the list is
   `MENU_PLACES` in `src/logic/places.ts`) with a glass **Table stays | Full** switch.
   Eight more panoramas were generated by the owner with ChatGPT, then upscaled the
   same way, horizon levelled and zenith calmed (`tools/make-places.py … <horizon°>`,
   `cap … <r,g,b>`); seven ship (lake terrace, tea garden, meadow, desert, cove, snow
   cabin, olive terrace), the forest awaits a regenerated source (TECH.md §16). The
   tea garden has **in-engine rain** (`src/desk/rain.ts`: three distance layers, eave
   drips, splashes, pond ripples, spatial rain sound; 3 draw calls, zero CPU per frame;
   you stay sheltered under the veranda roof).

The lamp is the agent: it breathes at rest, brightens while listening, a band of
light turns inside it while it thinks, and it **speaks**: 76 short lines
(every phase instruction and feedback line) in a calm, warm voice, played as a
positional sound from the lamp, its glow following the loudness of the words.
Captions always stay. "Voice" button under "Places" (or "voice off" / "voice on")
mutes it; the choice is remembered. The lamp never talks over you (its line waits
until you stop) and the microphone ignores it while it talks.
**Voice provenance:** Kokoro-82M v1.0 (Apache-2.0), voice `af_heart`, rendered
offline on the Mac by `tools/make-voice.mjs` (kokoro-js, Apache-2.0) and checked
line by line with Whisper base.en; no paid call, nothing generated at runtime.
If the files are missing, the browser's `speechSynthesis` is used when present
([unverified on Quest Browser]).
Typeface: Manrope (SIL Open Font License, `public/fonts/`).

## The evening and its rituals (v6)

- **The lamp proposes, you decide.** After the greeting the lamp proposes a
  ritual (`src/logic/evening.ts`, tested): late → Breathe; "tense" words →
  "You sound tense. Shall we breathe first?"; one heavy thing → "That sounds
  heavy. Shall we let it sink?" (what you said becomes the stone); a full head →
  Unload starts with it. After a ritual it chains: Unload → Release → Breathe →
  "Ready for sleep?". Nothing starts without "yes", a touch on a menu row or a
  brain dump. Menu: the "Rituals" ornament in the row in front of the tray
  (Rituals · Places · Voice; it reads "Finish" during Breathe and Release); rows Unload · Breathe · Release · Good night,
  each with how many evenings you did it, the proposed one in the lamp's light.
  Voice: "breathe", "release", "unload", "rituals", "yes", "not now",
  "that's enough", "longer", "let it go", "good night" (whole sentences only).
- **Breathe** (`src/logic/breathe.ts`): 4 s in, 6 s out, 6 breaths ("longer"
  adds 4, up to 12). The whole lamp (stoneware foot, brass ring, globe) glides
  to the centre of the tray and grows; its globe becomes a soft moon resting in
  its cup, swelling on the inhale and settling on the exhale; its warm light
  falls on the walnut and on your open hands; motes rise and fall; the room
  (passthrough or a Place) dims a little on each exhale, never the desk; a
  glass pearl lights for each breath. The voice guides the first two breaths
  and the last one, then lets silence and soft tones carry it. Optional:
  open your hands as you breathe in, close them as you breathe out (hand
  joints); a followed breath lights its pearl brighter. Ends gently at any
  time ("Finish", "that's enough"). Nothing moves you.
- **Release** (`src/logic/release.ts`): the bowls slide into the tray, a
  polished black marble bowl of still water rises; the lamp stays on its foot.
  The water mirrors the room's light (analytic planar reflection, no extra
  render) and lights up under the sinking stone.
  What weighs on you becomes a matte basalt stone engraved with ≤ 4 words
  (agent summary, `src/shared/agent/weight.ts`, `POST /api/summarize`). Pinch
  it, hold it over the water, open your fingers: it sinks with luminous
  ripples and warm bubbles, the engraving fills with light and dissolves.
  "Gone. Breathe out." Voice alternative: "let it go". A drop beside the
  water just brings the stone back. No microphone: touch the lamp to use
  tonight's heaviest stone.
- **Reason to come back:** each evening keeps its rituals, breaths and sunk
  stones (each one also a star) on the device.

## Code map

- `src/logic/` pure, unit-tested: desk layout and snapping, session and undo,
  table choice, voice intents, ritual state machine, memory, VAD, head gaze.
- `src/shared/agent/` agent shared by server and browser: simulated agent,
  prompts, defensive JSON parsing, LLM agent with fallback.
- `server/` Node built-ins only: providers, routes, rate limit, body limits.
- `src/systems/ritual-system.ts` the ECS system; `src/desk/` rendering
  (shaders with baked lighting, canvas typography, light field, sounds).
- `src/desk/design-tokens.ts` art direction and 2D tokens: one warm colour
  language (oiled walnut tray with a raised rim, speckled stoneware bowls with
  engraved names, grey river stones with engraved text, a small opal lamp), warm visionOS-style glass, type sizes
  derived from the visionOS HIG (17 pt default / 12 pt minimum, converted to
  millimetres at the viewing distance, ×1.35 for Quest), 4.4 cm targets, springs.
- `src/desk/film-set.ts` emulator-only film set (see below).
- `tools/` sound synthesis, emulator probes, perf probe, evening capture.
- v6: `src/desk/ritual-visuals.ts`, `ritual-materials.ts`, `ritual-geometry.ts`
  (black bowl, water, pearls, palm glow, room dimmer); `tools/capture-rituals-v6.mjs`,
  `tools/stills-rituals-v6.sh`, `tools/perf-rituals-v6.mjs`.

### Film set (emulator only)

On Quest the tray sits on your real table in passthrough. In the emulator the room is
a grey low-poly scan, so `src/desk/film-set.ts` replaces it **only in `vite dev` and only
when the IWER emulator is present**: a 360° photograph of a room graded to evening, and
an oak table top fitted to the detected table plane. It is a dynamic import behind
`import.meta.env.DEV`, so it is not in `dist/`. Options: `?set=off`, `?photo=fireplace`,
`?rot=<radians>`; at runtime `__wdd.film.fov(deg)`, `__wdd.film.room(rot, exposure)`,
`__wdd.film.hide()`, `__wdd.film.photo(name)`, `__wdd.film.history(evenings)` (film only:
weeks of seeded history so the video can show the growing sky; said on screen), and
`__wdd.arrive()` replays the arrival (the lamp wakes, then the desk).

Credits (CC0, Poly Haven): "Hotel Room" and "Fireplace" HDRIs by Greg Zaal (tone-mapped
JPGs, `film-set/`), "American Walnut Veneer" (v7: the film-set table, `film-set/walnut-table.jpg`;
the v6 tray copy is archived in `archive/textures-v6/`) and "White Oak Veneer" (v6 film-set table,
`film-set/oak-table.jpg`) textures.

**v7 tray: real oak.** "Oak Veneer 01" by Jenelle van Heerden (Poly Haven, CC0), a 4K photo-scan
(albedo, OpenGL normal, roughness; MD5 checked against the Poly Haven API). `tools/make-oak.mjs` crops
0.457 × 0.915 m of it (the whole tray, no tiling, 22 px/cm) to `public/textures/oak-albedo.ktx2`
(ETC1S, mipmapped, 417 KB) and `oak-nrm-rough.ktx2` (normal xy + roughness, 512 × 1024 UASTC + zstd,
541 KB), plus an 18 KB JPG preview shown until they are transcoded. GPU memory ≈ 2.0 MB with mips
(the v6 walnut JPG took 2.7 MB). The floor, the long rim pieces and the end pieces each read their own
part of the board with the grain along the piece; the mitred corners show darker end grain and a fine seam.
