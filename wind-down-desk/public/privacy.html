Privacy

An evening ritual is private. Here is everything that happens to what you say, in plain words.

Your voice

When you talk with the lamp, the short clip of what you just said is sent to Groq, which turns it into text. Our server only passes it along: it never keeps the recording.

Choose Quiet evening and the microphone is never used at all.

Your words

The text of what you said is sent to Google Gemini, which turns it into the stones on your desk and the small plan for tomorrow. Our server keeps none of it.

Groq and Google handle these requests under their own terms, which may let them keep them for a while: Groq privacy policy, Gemini API terms. If they cannot be reached, a simpler helper runs on your device instead and nothing leaves it.

Talk it through

When you choose Talk it through, what you say is sent to Google Gemini so the lamp can answer, and the lamp's answer is sent to Google Gemini again to be read aloud in its voice. Only a short summary of the talk and the last few exchanges are sent each time. Our server keeps none of it. On Google's free tier, Google may use this content to improve its products.

If you speak of hurting yourself or of ending your life, the lamp does not answer with the model: it shows free helplines and stays with you.

On your device

Your stones, your plan for tomorrow, what you said was done, the stars of what you let go, where your desk stands and your settings are kept in this browser only, on this headset. Nobody else can see them. The lamp uses them to remember: “Last night you let go of 3 things. Did you call the dentist?”

Where your desk stands and your accessibility settings stay, so the desk still finds your table.

The visit counter

To learn whether the demo works, the page counts a few anonymous steps, for example:

  • the desk was placed, a ritual started, a place was chosen;
  • the kind of device and browser (for example “Quest, Meta browser”).

Never your words, never your voice, never your IP address. No cookies, no advertising, no tracking across other sites. A visit is only a one-way code that cannot be turned back into who you are.

Not therapy or medical advice. Wind-Down Desk is a calm way to end the day. It does not replace a doctor or a therapist. If you are struggling, please reach out to someone you trust or to a local helpline.
